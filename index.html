ROLE Digital Designer · Creative Systems
CLIENT CareerPaddy (EdTech)

Spreadsheet-Driven Production Pipeline & Curriculum Mapping 1,397 Catalog Lessons

The Constraint

Course production was bottlenecked by ad-hoc graphic creation for every individual lesson. The marketing team needed to launch dozens of video courses weekly without sacrificing brand consistency.

The Modular System

Constructed a locked typographic grid with dynamic 3-tier color coding (Emerald for Foundations, Gold for Intermediate, Cyan for Advanced) mapped directly to curriculum spreadsheets.

The Outcome

Accelerated production turnaround by 30%, successfully deployed 46+ video assets, and enabled non-design team members to orchestrate catalog launches autonomously.
