Ektos

A finance interface exploration: helping people find their way from everyday money management to savings and budgeting.

UI concept / Interface design

THE ONBOARDING FLOW

One step at a time.

The opening screens introduce the value of the product before the sign-in and account-creation steps. Explore each screen at your own pace.

01 — Take Control of Your Finances

Introduces the core value proposition: understanding and managing everyday finances.

02 — Save Towards Your Goal

Turns financial intentions into visible, trackable savings goals.

03 — Budget With Confidence

Introduces budgeting as a practical system for staying on top of spending.

04 — Sign In

A familiar, focused authentication flow that brings users back into the product.

05 — Create Account

A simple entry point into the Ektos experience.

These are interface concepts. This presentation does not represent a shipped app or a tested prototype.
